# MYSTIC

A browser creature-collecting RPG. Explore, meet beasts, battle them, fuse them
into mutants, and evolve them.

**Play:** https://truongnvo-cmd.github.io/mystic/

## What's in it

- **Beasts grown from a seed.** Body plan, element, parts, colours and stats are
  all derived from one number, so the roster is effectively unlimited.
- **22 rarity tiers**, from Common up to Origin.
- **Fusion.** Combine two beasts and the child is a *mutant* — it keeps almost
  everything both parents had, sometimes grows a part neither had, and comes out
  lopsided and two-toned with a seam down its body.
- **Evolution** at levels 12 and 26.
- **Elemental regions.** The land is divided into zones and what spawns in the
  grass depends on where you are standing.
- **Turn-based battles** with a type chart, items, and switching.
- **Hand-drawn sprite support.** Drop a `<name>_walk.png` or `<name>_attack.png`
  sprite sheet into `refs/` and the game slices it, keys out the background, and
  animates it.
- **One-time event.** A shrine holds a beast that cannot be caught, rolled or
  fused into existence. One save, one chance.

## Controls

Arrow keys to roam · **P** party · **F** fuse · **B** shop
